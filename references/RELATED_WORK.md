## Assistance and coding ingredients

### Smolin, Verstraete, and Winter (2005)

[Entanglement of assistance and multipartite state distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505038), Physical Review A **72**, 052317. Theorem 1 supplies the regularized minimum-entropy assistance result; Theorem 8 turns a fixed environmental measurement into a flagged channel and applies ordinary quantum coding. The paper also uses coherent superpositions and random coding. These methods and receiver-only assistance are inherited. Here a single collected mode is measured, inaccessible loss remains in the coherent information, and the construction is compared with the **optimized** number detector rather than a chosen input example. The inspected primary PDF was arXiv v1, Theorems 1 and 8 and the associated construction.

### Wilde and Qi (2018)

[Energy-constrained private and quantum capacities of quantum channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.01997v2), IEEE Transactions on Information Theory **64**, 7802–7827. Theorem 2 supplies energy-constrained coherent-information achievability; Theorem 3 identifies the single-letter capacity of a degradable channel under the Gibbs-observable and finite-output-entropy conditions. The counting channel satisfies those conditions. Finite-support coherent detectors satisfy them on their coded input spaces. The thermal-reference method in Section X is also inherited. The primary v2 statements, hypotheses, and relevant proof passages were inspected. They do not perform the present optimization over detector classes.

### Winter (2016)

[Tight uniform continuity bounds for quantum entropies: conditional entropy, relative entropy distance and energy constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07775v6), Communications in Mathematical Physics **347**, 291–313. Lemma 2 supplies continuity of conditional entropy with dependence on the finite reference dimension, not the conditioning-system dimension. It justifies the finite-record limit and the transmission converse. Thermal-tail convergence in the constructive argument is additionally derived from entropy-of-mixture bounds. The primary v6 lemma and proof were inspected.

The [resource account](../science/detector_resources.md) also applies Lemma 2 directly to the actually implemented receiver-plus-record channel, including unobserved apparatus information. Its message-rate converse combines the standard conditional mutual-information bound by classical message entropy with degradability and antidegradability. The unassisted pure-loss benchmark is inherited from Wilde and Qi, Eq. (1), Section X.A and Eqs. (331)–(332). The matched leading record cost and explicit finite-cutoff consequences are applications of those established ingredients, not new general entropy or pure-loss-capacity theorems. The Fourier-stage excitation account concerns the specified implementation and does not assert a universal apparatus-energy bound.

## Closest optical measurement comparisons

### Finite references and conserving implementations

Åberg, [Catalytic Coherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1060), Physical Review Letters **113**, 150402 (2014), gives the shift lift in Eq. (1) and treats the bounded-below ladder. Chiribella, Yang, and Renner, [Fundamental energy requirement of reversible quantum operations](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.10884), Physical Review X **11**, 021014 (2021), Section IV, supplies a finite sine reference and conserving total-energy-sector implementation. Appendix A, Corollary 3, and Appendix D establish the gate-energy lower bound and its diamond-error formulation. Navascués and Popescu, [How energy conservation limits our measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.2101), Physical Review Letters **112**, 140502 (2014), derives apparatus-dependent measurement constraints; Eq. (15) expresses the role of battery coherences and Appendix E gives the sine state.

These apparatus constructions and general energy theorems are inherited. The [finite-reference account](../science/finite_reference.md) specializes them to the optical detector, derives an explicit reduced-channel error bound, and combines it with the existing coding accuracy estimate. The matching energy order concerns the prescribed unitary, not all measurements or capacity-achieving detectors. The record tradeoff is a separate application of the classical-message converse and finite-cutoff detector; it is not inferred from a battery theorem.

The [direct capacity argument](../science/direct_reference.md) uses the same supplied sine state and conserving lift. Its entropy bound for mixtures of translated references is proved locally using a Dirichlet Laplacian and geometric maximum entropy. It gives linear sufficient reference stock at fixed additive communication-rate loss. The [local-reference ceiling](../science/reference_width_bounds.md) instead follows from conserved-number refinement, conditional receiver entropy, and a multinomial estimator. Its support-width consequence concerns the per-mode conservation model and energy-driven regime. Neither result is inferred from a gate-level energy lower bound, and no priority certification or universal mean-energy converse is asserted.

### Memarzadeh, Cafaro, and Mancini (2011)

[Quantum information reclaiming after amplitude damping](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.0497), Journal of Physics A **44**, 045304. Section 4.2, Eqs. (41)–(42) and (52)–(54), mixes zero- and two-photon environmental outcomes at equal magnitude and leaves the one-photon outcome unchanged. It improves corrected entanglement fidelity in the qutrit loss model with full environmental access. That detector and qualitative qubit/qutrit contrast precede the [finite-cutoff example](../science/qubit_optical_bridge.md). The latter comparison uses inaccessible residual loss and an achievable unconditional rate exceeding a converse for all counting codes. The primary loss model and relevant equations were inspected; this targeted comparison does not imply exhaustive priority certification.

### Quadratic-phase designs and Kerr phase measurements

Iosue, Sharma, Gullans, and Albert, [Continuous-variable quantum state designs: theory and applications](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.05127v3), Physical Review X **14**, 011013 (2024), Theorem C.9, supplies the finite quadratic-phase construction. Equation (15) gives number-squared Kerred phase vectors. Iosue, Mooney, Ehrenberg, and Gorshkov, [Projective toric designs, quantum state designs, and mutually unbiased bases](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13479v3), Quantum **8**, 1546 (2024), Theorem 2.7, presents the projective toric design and handles duplicate phase vectors; Proposition 2.13 gives the support-size bound. These designs, their fourth moments, and the Kerr/phase primitive are inherited. The local [proof](../science/coherent_measurements.md) applies them to the helper purity calculation, then selects one quadratic setting to retain the optical rate guarantee with linearly many coded outcomes. It does not claim a new design or efficient physical synthesis.

### Fiurášek and Mišta (2007)

[Gaussian localizable entanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2799v1), Physical Review A **75**, 060302(R). The paper optimizes Gaussian localization in specified state families and demonstrates an improvement using photon counting. Its final example, including Eq. (15), prepares Fock states by measuring a squeezed-state partner and splits them on a balanced beam splitter, producing binomial Schmidt entropies. Thus counting-induced entanglement and binomial-entropy behavior are prior art. That example does not optimize a quantum transmission channel over all mean-energy-constrained inputs, nor prove a uniform converse with inaccessible residual loss. The primary four-page text, including that example, was inspected.

### Lami, Takagi, and Adesso (2020)

[Assisted concentration of Gaussian resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.02173v2), Physical Review A **101**, 052305. Theorem 11 and Section VI compare Gaussian and general assistance for specified states when the helper holds a purification. For equal-temperature product thermal states, they give a high-energy constant gap between the Gaussian and unrestricted assisted entanglement values. Section VI also credits photon-counting advantages from earlier localization work. This is a substantive precedent for quantitative assistance comparisons, not the present counting-versus-coherent channel theorem: the resource restriction, source state, inaccessible subsystem, and optimized objective differ. The primary v2 theorem and Section VI were inspected; their qualitative message is not claimed as new here.

### Input-environment preparation is a different resource

Wang and Jiang, [Passive environment-assisted quantum communication with GKP states](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16781v2), Physical Review X **15**, 021003 (2025), improve transmission by preparing the *incoming* environment. Voss, Li, Wang, and Jiang, [Passive Environment-Assisted Quantum Communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21549v2) (2026), further study non-Gaussian ancilla inputs. Their primary abstracts identify this different access model. Here every unused input is vacuum and the helper only measures collected *outgoing* light. These sources were screened for their operational setup, not audited in full, and are not imported proof ingredients.

## What remains after attribution

The detector-capacity account combines residual-variance and thinning converses uniform over all finite-mean photon distributions, their match by a negative-binomial counting construction, a one-mode purity bound, and the resulting uniform energy/loss crossover and finite-energy capacity separation. Its structured detector application uses the inherited phase designs above and selects a single setting. The [two-photon example](../science/qubit_optical_bridge.md) supplies a certified finite-cutoff comparison with optimized counting. The elementary entropy inequalities are derived locally as tools, without a separate priority claim for those inequalities. The collective capacity, general assistance methodology, and phase-design constructions retain their source attribution.

The principal consequence distinguishes coherence *within* one collected mode from coherent processing *across* many modes. It does not assert that Gaussian detectors are optimal, that every non-Gaussian detector helps, or that the exhibited finite POVM has an efficient implementation.
